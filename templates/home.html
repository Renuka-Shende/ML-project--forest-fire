What is forest fire?

A forest fire, also known as a wildfire, is an uncontrolled fire that occurs in a forested area or vegetation-rich area. These fires can occur naturally due to lightning strikes or human activities such as campfires, discarded cigarettes, or arson. Forest fires can spread rapidly, consuming trees, shrubs, and other vegetation, and they can cause significant damage to ecosystems, wildlife habitats, property, and sometimes even human lives. Firefighters and emergency responders work to contain and extinguish forest fires to minimize their impact and prevent further damage.
